Store Policies

Clear terms for physical goods.

Fairchild Alchemy sells desk objects, kinetic curiosities, crafted home goods, ritual objects, and giftable instruments for adult spaces. Orders currently ship within the United States.

Shipping

Checkout collects a US shipping address and phone number. Ready-to-ship items are prepared in 3-7 business days unless the product page or follow-up message states otherwise.

Returns

Unused, undamaged physical goods may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Return shipping is the buyer's responsibility unless an item arrives damaged or incorrect.

Refunds & Disputes

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after returned goods are inspected. If something arrives damaged, contact us with photos within 7 days so we can resolve it before a dispute is needed.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before fulfillment begins. Limited, commissioned, customized, consumable, or used goods may be final sale once work or shipment has started.

Restrictions

Products are decorative, functional, or collectible objects for adults. They are not children's toys. Some sharp, fragile, heated, scented, or small-component items require careful handling.

Promotions

Any promotion, discount, or limited offer will state its own terms at the point of sale. Promotions cannot be applied retroactively unless stated in writing.
